## **Fixathon Theme**

For this Fixathon, we have a clear mission: 24 hours to build solutions that get us back on track!

Over a decade ago, the Paris Agreement set a clear goal: keep global heating well below 2°C, and pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5°C. Yet today, we're still far from delivering on those commitments. We know the problems and have many of the solutions. What we need is implementation. That's why, ahead of the forthcoming "implementation" climate conference in Turkey (COP31), we'll focus on how new data and tools, such as AI, can help us find and build solutions to the systemic challenges still hindering climate action.

**That's why we're gathering 50 multidisciplinary problem-solvers for 24 hours with one question: can AI help turn climate commitments into action?**

>
> 💡 **Note:** You can't work on existing projects. You'll need to build a new, innovative solution during the event itself, with your team, based on the Fixathon challenge and the tracks.
>

## Budget Support

If you're traveling from **outside of Barcelona**, we'll reimburse **up to €400 per person** for travel to and accommodation in Barcelona for the Fixathon.

- Reimbursement will happen **after the event **and only if you attend everything in person and submit valid receipts (PDFs from your booking/payment) related to the event. We will check your attendance for the entire event.
- This means you'll need to book and pay for flights/trains and accommodation yourself, and we'll send the budget support the week after the Fixathon.
- Anything not related to travel and accommodation expenses won't be covered by us, and we will only pay up to €400, as this is a fixed amount.
- To request the reimbursement, fill out the budget reimbursement form before the event.

>
> 💡 **Note:** If you arrive earlier or leave later that week, that's not a problem. We'll still count it as valid travel (flight/train tickets) to the event. For example, if you book a flight departing Sunday and returning Saturday, that's fine. However, we'll only accept hotel/accommodation receipts for the nights of **October 12th–16th**, since these are the dates directly connected to the event. If you book your hotel for longer, that's also fine, but make sure to only send us receipts/prices for the nights related to our event.

## **Teams & Matching**

The best teams combine different skills and perspectives. Diversity in background, thinking, and experience helps unlock stronger ideas and more impactful solutions.

We carefully reviewed all applications and matched teams primarily based on complementary skills, making sure every team has a strong mix of people and perspectives.

If you applied alone or as a team of two, we matched you with additional participants to complete your team. Teams consist of a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 4 people.
