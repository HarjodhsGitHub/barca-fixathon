# Impact/Week — Where to stay

Extracted from the neighborhood map in [Barcelona: The House, Hotels & Tips](barcelona-the-house-hotels-and-tips.md). The original PDF is [assets/barcelona-areas-map.pdf](assets/barcelona-areas-map.pdf).

Norrsken House Barcelona is in the southern part of area 1, Barceloneta.

## 1. Barceloneta and Passeig Marítim

- **Accommodation:** Several upscale beachfront hotels and some apartment rentals
- **Accessibility to Norrsken:** short walk
- **Liveliness:** Very lively, especially in summer; vibrant beach scene
- **Fancy level:** Moderate; beach casual with some upscale spots in Passeig Colom
- **Safety:** Generally safe; watch out for pickpockets

## 2. Ciutat Vella

- **Accommodation:** Boutique hotels, some luxury options and apartment rentals
- **Accessibility to Norrsken:** Easy; short walk or 20-min public transport
- **Liveliness:** Extremely lively; heart of the city's history and culture. Not to everyone's taste
- **Fancy level:** Mixed; historic charm and unruliness with a blend of budget and luxury
- **Safety:** Generally safe but crowded; be cautious of pickpockets

## 3. Arc de Triomf & Fort Pienc

- **Accommodation:** Limited big hotels; more mid-range options and apartment rentals
- **Accessibility to Norrsken:** Easy; short walk or 30-min public transport
- **Liveliness:** Moderately lively; family-friendly atmosphere
- **Fancy level:** Moderate; relaxed with some elegant parks and boulevards
- **Safety:** Very safe; well-maintained public spaces

## 4. Poblenou & Diagonal Mar

- **Accommodation:** Several large, modern hotels and apartment rentals
- **Accessibility to Norrsken:** Easy/Moderate; 30-40 min public transport or 20 min drive
- **Liveliness:** Mostly offices; quiet at night
- **Fancy level:** Moderate to high; modern urban vibe with upscale areas
- **Safety:** Safe and modern; less crowded

## 5. Eixample

- **Accommodation:** Luxurious hotels and many apartment rentals
- **Accessibility to Norrsken:** Moderate; 30-50 min public transport or 20-30 min drive
- **Liveliness:** Busy, upscale area; modernist architecture
- **Fancy level:** High; upscale shopping and dining, elegant architecture
- **Safety:** Very safe; residential and tourist-friendly

## 6. Sant Antoni, Paral·lel & Poble Sec

- **Accommodation:** Fewer large hotels; more boutique and budget options
- **Accessibility to Norrsken:** Easy/Moderate; 30-40 min public transport or 20 min drive
- **Liveliness:** Trendy and up-and-coming; bustling with locals
- **Fancy level:** Moderate/trendy for Sant Antoni; Low for Paral·lel and Poble Sec
- **Safety:** Safe and friendly; vibrant local community. Paral·lel and Poble Sec can be unruly

## 7. Raval

We recommend to avoid this area.
