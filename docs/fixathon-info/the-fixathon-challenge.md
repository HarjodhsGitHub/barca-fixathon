## Challenge: Fixing the Paris Agreement

**COP21 → COP31: 24h to build solutions to get us back on track**

Ten years ago, the Paris Agreement set a clear goal: keep global heating well below 2°C, and pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5°C. Yet today, we're still far from delivering on those commitments. We know the problems. We have many of the solutions. What we need is implementation.

Ahead of COP31, we're handing that challenge over to you: explore how AI, data, and open tools can help turn climate commitments into real-world action.

You have 24 hours in Barcelona, in a room full of talent dedicated to accelerating the climate transition. One goal: leverage AI to get us back on track. **Let's prompt what matters! **

---

### Track 1: Unblock, implement, and scale existing solutions

Countries and industries already have climate plans, from national NDCs to sector roadmaps, that spell out where change is needed. We also have a mass of technologies and capital ready to deploy. Yet the two rarely meet, because implementing and deploying the right solution for a specific need in a specific area is often slow, bureaucratic, and highly politicized.

**Can AI translate climate plans into concrete needs and connect them with ready-to-scale solutions?**

Use climate plans, policy data, and other relevant sources to identify specific needs and match them with solutions that exist today. Your prototype might surface regional or sectoral patterns, recommend technologies, highlight deployment opportunities, or show where no suitable solution exists yet.

**The goal is to make the path from need to deployment faster and smarter. **

---

### Track 2: Leverage data to empower more effective climate action

We know a great deal about where emissions come from, how they change over time, and which sources or activities are the most carbon-intensive. Despite this, decision-makers and consumers are often faced with conflicting data, and different parties seek to influence the public debate. **The challenge is turning data into better decisions and concrete action!**

Use emissions data to build something that helps people identify what matters, where the biggest opportunities lie to cut emissions, and what to do next. Your solution could detect emissions hotspots, uncover patterns, or highlight reduction opportunities. It could also be a scalable consumer product that shows where action has the greatest impact for people and planet, or that helps a specific user make better decisions.

**The key question is: what can someone decide or do because your solution exists that they could not do the week before?**

Some inspiration: [AI Footprint Calculator](https://aifootprintcalculator.org/) (by Klimatkollen, Unearth & 2050)

---

### Track 3: Choose your own climate challenge

Not every important climate problem or solution fits neatly into a predefined challenge. If you see another opportunity for AI, data, or technology to make a difference, this is your track.

Start with a real climate problem and build something that could help solve it. You might rethink an existing process, remove a barrier, create a new tool, or combine technologies and data in a way that hasn't been tried before.

The direction is yours. But your solution should have a clear user, a well-defined problem, and a credible path to real-world impact.

**Think boldly. Build fast. Show us what's possible. **

## Data sources and tools

>
> 💡 Below is a curated list of data sources to get you started, plus a few open-source tools for analysing and mapping data. They are grouped by theme, so you can find what you need. Take some time before Barcelona to explore them, but remember that they are here to guide you, not to limit you.
>

#### Climate Policy, Targets & Needs

- [**UNFCCC – NDC Registry**](https://unfccc.int/NDCREG)**: **Nationally Determined Contributions submitted by countries under the Paris Agreement.
- [**EU National Energy and Climate Plans (NECPs)**](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/energy-strategy/national-energy-and-climate-plans-necps_en)**: **National plans outlining how EU countries intend to meet climate and energy targets across areas such as energy, transport, industry and buildings.
- [**Climate Policy Radar**](https://www.climatepolicyradar.org/)**: **Searchable global database of climate laws, policies, strategies and commitments, including NDCs.

#### Climate Solutions & Innovation

- [**Sweden – The Climate Matchmaker**](https://explore.sweden.se/sweden-the-climate-matchmaker)**: **Swedish climate solutions across areas including energy, industry, transport, buildings, circularity and agriculture.
- [**Project Drawdown**](https://drawdown.org/)**: **Research and analysis of existing climate technologies and solutions across sectors.
- [**CORDIS – EU Research & Innovation Projects**](https://cordis.europa.eu/projects)**: **Database of EU-funded research and innovation projects, including climate and clean-tech solutions.
- [**EU Innovation Fund – Project Portfolio**](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/eu-action/eu-funding-climate-action/innovation-fund/innovation-fund-projects_en)**: **European projects demonstrating and deploying innovative low-carbon technologies.

#### Emissions Data

- [**Climate TRACE**](https://climatetrace.org/)**: **Detailed global greenhouse-gas emissions data covering countries, sectors and individual emission sources.
- [**EDGAR – Emissions Database for Global Atmospheric Research**](https://edgar.jrc.ec.europa.eu/)**: **Global greenhouse-gas emissions data by country, sector and geographic area from the European Commission's Joint Research Centre.
- [**European Industrial Emissions Portal**](https://industry.eea.europa.eu/)**: **Data on emissions and environmental performance from large industrial facilities across Europe.
- [**Eurostat – Environment, Energy & Economy Data**](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/)**: **European statistics covering emissions, energy, transport, industry, population and economic activity.
- [**Klimatkollen**](https://klimatkollen.se/en/)**: **NGO publishing corporate emissions data for over 2,000 companies.

#### Satellite Imagery & Earth Observation

- [**ESA / Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem**](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/)**: **Sentinel satellite imagery and Earth observation data.
- [**MethaneSAT**](https://www.methanesat.org/)**: **The first non-profit methane-tracking satellite mission developed to provide data from the global oil and gas industry.

#### Energy & Infrastructure Data

- [**Ember**](https://ember-energy.org/data/)**: **Global electricity data covering generation, demand, capacity, carbon intensity and power-sector emissions.
- [**Global Energy Monitor**](https://globalenergymonitor.org/)**: **Open data on energy and industrial infrastructure, including power plants, renewable energy, oil and gas and heavy industry.
- [**PVGIS – Photovoltaic Geographical Information System**](https://re.jrc.ec.europa.eu/pvg_tools/en/)**: **Solar irradiation and PV performance data for almost any location, from the European Commission's Joint Research Centre.
- [**OpenStreetMap**](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)**: **Open global map data including building footprints, roads, land use and energy infrastructure such as power lines and solar installations.

#### Companies, Trade & Supply Chains

- [**GLEIF – Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation**](https://www.gleif.org/)**: **Open register of legal entities and their parent-subsidiary relationships. Useful for linking company emissions data to corporate groups.
- [**OpenCorporates**](https://opencorporates.com/)**: **Open database of company registry data from jurisdictions worldwide.
- [**SEC EDGAR Full-Text Search**](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/)**: **Search US company filings for climate-related disclosures, transition risks and emissions targets.
- [**UN Comtrade**](https://comtradeplus.un.org/)**: **Global trade flows by product and country. Useful for tracing carbon-intensive imports and supply chain dependencies.
- [**USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries**](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/mineral-commodity-summaries)**: **Annual production, reserves and supply data for critical minerals used in batteries, solar panels and wind turbines.
- [**World Bank Commodity Markets (Pink Sheet)**](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets)**: **Monthly price data for energy, metals and agricultural commodities.

#### Open-Source Tools

- [**GeoPandas**](https://geopandas.org/)**: **Geospatial analysis.
- [**Shapely**](https://shapely.readthedocs.io/)**: **Spatial operations.
- [**Folium**](https://python-visualization.github.io/folium/) **/** [**Kepler.gl**](https://kepler.gl/)**: **Mapping and geospatial visualisation.
- [**PyOD**](https://pyod.readthedocs.io/)**: **Anomaly detection.

#### Scientific Reports and Climate Data

- [**IPCC**](https://www.ipcc.ch/)
- [**IPCC Data Distribution Centre**](https://www.ipcc-data.org/)
- [**NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis**](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/)
- [**Copernicus Climate Data Store**](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/)
- [**European Climate Risk Assessment / Climate Risk Map**](https://european-crt.org/map.html)
- [**NOAA Climate Data Online**](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/cdo-web/)
- [**Meteostat**](https://meteostat.net/)**: **Historical weather and climate data from weather stations worldwide.
- [**OpenWeather**](https://openweathermap.org/api): Weather API with current, forecast and historical data including cloud cover and UV index.
