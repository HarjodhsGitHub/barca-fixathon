# Budget Reimbursement Form

Source: https://app.notion.com/p/norrskenfoundation/Fixathon-at-Impact-Week-3994b2eeb66380f39ce7f4d71299369c

If you're traveling to the Fixathon from outside Barcelona, we cover your travel and accommodation in Barcelona, up to €400 per person.

Please fill out the form **before Barcelona**, so we can start the process right after the event.

>
> 👉 **Link to the form: **[**Budget Reimbursement Form**](https://norrsken.typeform.com/to/o4i2edFg)
>

#### **Some notes on the form and the process:**

- **Attendance:** To get the reimbursement, you have to attend the Fixathon in person. We will check attendance.
- **What the form asks for:** Your contact details, bank details, and receipts. Receipts must show your full name, the dates, and the price (PDFs from your booking or payment).
- **Your receipts:** Please merge all travel receipts into one PDF, and all accommodation receipts into one PDF. Name each file with your name, for example "FirstName_LastName_Travel.pdf" and "FirstName_LastName_Accommodation.pdf".
- **When you get paid:** We aim to start the reimbursement process on Friday, October 23.
- **Your data:** It will be deleted once all payments are completed.
