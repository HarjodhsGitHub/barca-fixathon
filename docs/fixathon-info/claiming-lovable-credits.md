# Claiming Lovable Credits

Source: https://app.notion.com/p/norrskenfoundation/Fixathon-at-Impact-Week-3994b2eeb66380f39ce7f4d71299369c

Every Fixathon participant gets **300 free Lovable credits**. Below is a step-by-step guide on how to claim them during the event.

>
> 💡 **Before the Fixathon:** You need a Lovable account to claim your credits, so please create one in advance if you don't have one yet.
>

#### **New to Lovable?**

- Lovable ([lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev/)) is an AI app builder. You describe what you want in plain language, and it builds a working app or website that you can keep improving by chatting with it.
- To get started, check out their official [documentation](https://docs.lovable.dev/) and explore [step-by-step guides and video tutorials](https://lovable.dev/guides).

#### **How it works on October 13th**

1. **Get your QR code:** You'll receive your unique code from the Norrsken team during check-in in Barcelona. Each code gives you 300 credits.
2. **Scan and log in:** Scan the code with your phone and sign in to your Lovable account.
3. **Choose your workspace:** Select where you want your credits added.

>
> ❗ **Don't lose your QR code!** We only have as many codes as there are Fixathon participants, so we can't replace one if it gets lost. Keep it safe once you receive it!
>

#### **Working in a team?**

- Each code can only be redeemed once, and each workspace can only receive credits once. So rather than creating a new shared workspace, build together in one project and invite your teammates to collaborate on it.
