## **About <Norrsken>**

[**Norrsken**](https://www.norrsken.org/) is a non-profit, non-partisan and non-religious foundation, started by Niklas Adalberth, co-founder of Klarna. We exist because we believe entrepreneurship and new technology are two of the most powerful forces we have for solving the world’s biggest challenges. We want to get the best founders working on problems that matter, and surround them with the capital, knowledge and network to actually pull it off.

Today, we run Norrsken Houses in **Stockholm**, **Barcelona**, **Kigali**, **Brussels** and **Amsterdam**, and a family of funds backing entrepreneurs who pair real returns with real-world results.

## **Origin of the Fixathon**

We're living through the most profound shift since the Industrial Revolution. But this time, the engine of change isn't steam. It's intelligence. Yet we've barely scratched the surface of what this technology can truly do.

**AI is probably the most powerful tool humanity has ever held**. The question is what we choose to do with it. Let's not waste it on another sales agent. We need bigger prompts. We're calling on a new generation of problem engineers, who will use this divine intelligence to tackle what matters: our climate, our health, our food systems, our future.

That's why Norrsken brought the **Prompt What Matters** initiative to life and started hosting Fixathons at the end of 2025. Fixathons are Norrsken's take on a hackathon: teaming up for 24-hour sprints to leverage AI and try to fix some of the world's greatest challenges.

You can read more about the **Prompt What Matters** initiative and the previous **Fixathons** below!
