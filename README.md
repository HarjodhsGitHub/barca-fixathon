# Norrsken Hackathon

This is the repo for the team of David, Logan, Edvin and Harjodh for the Norrsken Hackathon in Barcelona.

We are doing task: Track 1 — Unblock, implement, and scale existing solutions

One-liner: A Global Burden of Disease for climate action — one euro value that lets a city compare every adaptation, mitigation and nature project, pick the best mix for its budget, see how it moves the city toward its bioregional, national and Paris goals, and get it delivered.

## Event

Norrsken Fixathon at Impact/Week, 13–15 October 2026, Norrsken House Barcelona.
