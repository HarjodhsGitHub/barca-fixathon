Recommended order changes for October
  1. Plasterboard 12.5 mm: cut the buffer from +14% to +6% and split deliveries per floor. −€312k/yr
  2. Formwork ply 18 mm: track reuse cycles (3.1 → 5) and lower the buffer to +9%. −€286k/yr
  3. Ready-mix C30/37: order the final pour in 0.25 m³ steps with a call-off truck. −€268k/yr
  4. C24 battens: lower the buffer from +18% to +8% and add a supplier return clause. −€234k/yr
  5. Rebar B500S: switch to supplier cut-and-bend schedules. −€172k/yr
  6. Mineral wool + packaging: lower the buffer to +5% and add pallet and wrap take-back. −€148k/yr
Method

Volumes come from calibrated depth and LiDAR scans of each skip, with an estimated ±6% error. Items are classified by the vision layer and labelled by an autoregressive model, which answers typed questions (material, fill level, order line, reusability, contamination) with probabilities. Recommendations are optimized against a 98.5% service-level constraint and backtested on 4,812 historic order lines.
